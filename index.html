About Antonio's

About Antonio's Cucina Italiana

Antonio's Cucina Italiana is Alturas' premier Italian restaurant serving authentic, house-made Italian cuisine — specialties include hand-tossed pizzas, fresh pasta in marinara, alfredo, and olive oil sauces, house salads with world-renowned dressings, hot subs, burgers, and more. When you dine at Antonio's, you are family. Whatever the occasion, Antonio's makes it one to remember. Fresh new look, fresh ingredients, fresh taste — a gift from Italy. We look forward to your visit!

Stan the owner and our entire team treat every customer like family. Come in for dine-in, call ahead for takeout, or order for delivery. We're open Monday through Sunday, 11AM to 9PM.
